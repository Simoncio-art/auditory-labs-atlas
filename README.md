# Auditory Atlas — Global Map & Connected-Labs Network

Interactive world map, Connected-Papers-style collaboration & bibliographic similarity graph, and searchable directory of **188 verified auditory neuroscience laboratories** across **29 countries** and **28 US states**.

- **Live Web App:** https://simoncio-art.github.io/auditory-labs-atlas/
- **Dataset Snapshot (2026-11-19):** 188 labs (121 established centers + 67 specialized/emerging groups), 491 verified coauthorship pairs, and 750 bibliographic/topic similarity bridges computed over 13,563 publications (2014–2026) verified via **OpenAlex**, **Europe PMC**, and **ORCID**.
- **Bilingual (EN / ES) & Direct Lab Links:** Automatically adapts to English or Spanish from your browser language (with a header switcher) and includes a one-click **Copy Link** button on every laboratory card to share a direct view of any lab.
- **Note on "ABR + MRI + Aging Match (%)":** This index strictly reflects thematic keyword overlap with auditory brainstem/ABR, neuroimaging/MRI, and aging/tinnitus — **not** a measure of lab quality or scientific prestige.
- **Privacy:** Your shortlists, theme and language preferences, and personal application notes stay locally in your browser and are never uploaded or shared.
